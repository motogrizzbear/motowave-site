Privacy

MotoWave Privacy Policy

MotoWave does not require an account and does not include ads, tracking, analytics SDKs, or a MotoWave backend.

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Data stored on your device

MotoWave stores favorite stations, song history, recently played stations, cached station lists and artwork, and app settings locally on your device.

You can clear song history, station cache, and artwork cache from Settings. Deleting the app removes its local app data.

Radio Browser

MotoWave uses the public Radio Browser service to find stations. Search terms, selected filters, station list requests, station click reports, and station votes may be sent to Radio Browser so the directory can return results and maintain community station statistics.

Audio streams

When you play a station, MotoWave connects directly to that station’s stream URL. The station or its hosting provider may receive normal network information such as your IP address, user agent, request time, and the stream requested. MotoWave does not control those third-party station servers.

AirPlay and Google Cast

AirPlay is provided through Apple system frameworks.

Google Cast uses your local network to discover compatible receivers. The Google Cast SDK references Bluetooth and microphone discovery APIs. MotoWave does not record audio. When you use Cast, Google and the Cast receiver may process the network activity needed to discover the device and play the selected stream.

What MotoWave does not do

MotoWave does not sell personal data, track you across apps or websites, create user profiles, use advertising identifiers, or upload your favorites, history, or cache to MotoWave servers.

This website

This public website provides product information, support documentation, and this privacy policy. It does not receive your MotoWave favorites, history, or settings.

The site does not intentionally add analytics, ads, tracking pixels, or cookies. If you choose a light or dark appearance override, that preference is stored locally in your browser. The site is hosted with GitHub Pages, so GitHub may process ordinary web requests and security logs under its own terms and privacy practices.

Changes

If MotoWave’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the related app update is released.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, email motowave@motobear.dev.
