A few answers

Simple app, simple policy.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. MotoWave is free. If you like it, you can buy me a coffee, but it does not unlock anything.

Where does my data go?

Your favorites, listening history, caches, and settings stay on your device. Station searches and playback still contact the public radio directory and station servers needed to make the app work. See the privacy policy for details.

Why does a station sometimes fail?

MotoWave plays streams provided by independent stations. Stations can go offline, change stream addresses, or have temporary network problems.
